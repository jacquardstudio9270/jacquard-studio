ABOUT JACQUARD STUDIO

Creating Textile Designs That Inspire

Jacquard Studio specializes in creating original textile designs for home textiles and furnishing fabrics. We develop premium-quality artwork for manufacturers, exporters, and brands looking for distinctive, production-ready textile collections.

Our portfolio includes Jacquard Fabrics, Woven Designs, Printed Fabrics, Embroidery, Jacquard Towels, and Bedding collections. Every design is created with creativity, commercial appeal, and manufacturing practicality in mind.
